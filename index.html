Frequently Asked Questions

What does an interior designer in Naples do?

An interior designer plans how a space looks and works. That includes layouts, finishes, furniture, lighting, color and styling, along with coordinating the people and deliveries needed to bring the plan to life. At Pure Design we manage this entire journey so you do not have to piece it together yourself.

How do I get started with Pure Design?

Simply call us to schedule a complimentary initial consultation. We will talk about your project, your style and your timeline, and explain how we would approach the work.

Can you design custom furniture?

Yes. When a layout is unusual or a specific look is needed, we design custom pieces and work with reputable vendors and artists to produce them with quality materials and careful craftsmanship.

Do you handle the full project, or only the design?

We can do both. Many clients rely on us for complete project management and installation, while others prefer design guidance alone. We shape our involvement around what you need.

Do you work with builders and contractors?

Absolutely. Our team understands construction as well as design, and we regularly coordinate with builders and trades so the design intent is protected from planning through completion.

Which areas do you serve?

We are based in Naples, Florida, and design homes for clients throughout the Naples area. Contact us to confirm availability for your location.
